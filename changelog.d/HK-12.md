### Security
- Claude Code's server-managed settings (set in the claude.ai admin console) are now read from Claude Code's local cache, `~/.claude/remote-settings.json`. Their deny and ask rules always win, `allowManagedPermissionRulesOnly` is honored wherever it appears in the cache, and when the cache holds a policy, allow rules from `managed-settings.json` no longer count, as in Claude Code. If the cache exists but can't be read, Lagom doesn't rewrite commands.
